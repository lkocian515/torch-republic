# Torch Republic QA & SEO Cleanup Case Study

## Project focus
Torch Republic needed a cleaner, more trustworthy storefront experience. The work centered on consistency, search hygiene, compliant product language, and conversion-readiness across the coffee, mushroom coffee, and matcha catalog.

## What changed
- Standardized the free-shipping message to $50+ across storefront copy, policy pages, metadata, and email campaign copy.
- Removed conflicting seven-versus-twelve catalog messaging.
- Protected administrative and internal routes with noindex handling or redirects.
- Removed legacy apparel and men’s collection exposure from public navigation and legacy route handling.
- Corrected the shop title from “Mushroom Shop” to the full coffee, mushroom coffee, and matcha category title.
- Corrected the bundle naming from “Mushroom Bundle” to ritual-focused bundle language.
- Replaced admin-facing contact language with a customer-facing support address.
- Removed or softened outdated and over-strong wellness claims.
- Updated dated comparison naming so evergreen content does not appear stale.
- Consolidated redundant SEO signals and documented QA corrections.

## Measurable results
- 0 remaining code/content matches for the old $75 or $45 shipping thresholds.
- 0 remaining matches for seven-versus-twelve product-count messaging.
- 0 remaining matches for “Mushroom Shop” and “Mushroom Bundle” legacy title strings.
- 0 remaining matches for admin@torchrepublic.com.
- 0 remaining matches for the flagged strong wellness phrases.
- 0 remaining matches for the dated “Premium Instant Coffee vs. K-Cups: Honest Comparison (2025)” title.

## Remaining verification needs
Some items require merchant-provided evidence or live QA rather than code cleanup alone:
- Verified product-information matrix from final product labels, serving counts, ingredients, and packaging details.
- Package-image confirmation against the exact product customers receive.
- Real product photography assets.
- Mobile purchase and bundle-flow testing through the Testing Agent or a live QA pass.
