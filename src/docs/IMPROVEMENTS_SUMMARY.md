## 1. New Homepage Sections (In Order)

### 1.1 Choose Your Ritual
**Location:** After reviews, before What's Inside the Tube
**Purpose:** Help customers choose blends based on desired outcome
**Features:**
- 5 ritual cards: Focus, Energy, Calm, Evening Ritual, Daily Balance
- Each card shows: best time to drink, taste profile, what it supports
- Clear Shop button for each blend
- Premium card design with icon, accent colors, and hover effects

### 1.2 What's Inside Every Cup
**Location:** After Choose Your Ritual
**Purpose:** Transparent ingredient and specification information
**Features:**
- 6 spec cards with icons:
  - Caffeine per Serving (~100mg)
  - Mushroom Blend Amount (500mg)
  - Servings per Tube (27)
  - Format (Instant Powder)
  - Functional Mushrooms (Lion's Mane, Chaga)
  - Extract Type (Dual Extract)
- Clean, science-aware design
- Footer note about gluten-free, dairy-free, no artificial additives

### 1.3 Tastes Like Coffee, Not Mushrooms
**Location:** After What's Inside Every Cup
**Purpose:** Address taste concerns and reassure first-time buyers
**Features:**
- 4 reassurance points:
  - Tastes Like Coffee (roasty, smooth, coffee-forward)
  - No Mushroom Chunks (fine powder, no texture)
  - Mixes Easily (hot water, milk, creamer)
  - Hot or Iced (works both ways)
- Customer testimonial box
- Warm, reassuring copy

### 1.4 Subscribe & Save 20%
**Location:** After Bundle Section
**Purpose:** Make subscription offer impossible to miss
**Features:**
- Prominent headline: "Subscribe & Save 20%"
- Clear value proposition: "Your daily mushroom coffee ritual, delivered automatically"
- 5 benefit bullets with checkmarks
- 4 trust badges:
  - 20% Off Every Order
  - Cancel Anytime
  - Pause or Skip
  - Secure Checkout
- CTA button to subscription page

### 1.5 Founder Trust
**Location:** After How to Use
**Purpose:** Human brand story without being overly personal
**Features:**
- Headline: "Created for the Intentional Drinker"
- Story box explaining the brand mission
- 4 core values:
  - Intentional Ritual
  - Functional Wellness
  - Clean Ingredients
  - Community First
- Professional, trustworthy tone

### 1.6 First Cup Guarantee
**Location:** After Founder Trust
**Purpose:** Risk-free trial reassurance
**Features:**
- Badge: "First Cup Guarantee"
- Headline: "Try Torch Republic Risk-Free"

## 4. Compliant Wellness Language

### 4.1 Approved Structure/Function Claims
**Use these phrases:**
- "Supports focus"
- "Supports calm energy"
- "Supports daily wellness"
- "Supports immune wellness"
- "Supports a balanced routine"
- "Supports mental clarity"
- "Supports balanced energy"

### 4.2 Prohibited Claims (DO NOT USE)
**Avoid these words:**
- Cure, treat, heal
- Reduce anxiety, ADHD, depression
- Reduce inflammation
- Autoimmune
- Alzheimer's
- Weight loss
- Detox
- Hormone balancing

### 4.3 FDA Disclaimer
**Include on product pages:**
> *These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Individual results may vary. Always consult with a healthcare professional before starting any new supplement regimen.*
