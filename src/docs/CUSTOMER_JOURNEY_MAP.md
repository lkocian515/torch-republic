# Torch Republic Customer Journey Map

## Overview
A seamless 3-step progression from discovery to subscription, with the collectible system reinforced throughout.

---

## The Complete Journey

### **STEP 1: DISCOVERY & INTEREST**
**Location:** Homepage  
**Primary Goal:** Introduce brand and capture attention with featured product

**Flow:**
```
Homepage Hero
  ↓
"Built for Those Who Carry the Flame" messaging
  ↓
Featured Product Spotlight (Washington Blend)
  ↓
Two Clear CTAs:
  1. "Start Here: Add to Cart" → Single Product Purchase
  2. "Join Torch Box → Never Miss a Blend" → Subscription
```

**Components Involved:**
- HeroSection: Brand authority, "Est. 2024"
- FeaturedProductSpotlight: Step 1 label, dual CTAs
- FirstTimeGuidance: Recommended starting product

**Reinforcement:**
- "This is your entry point" messaging
- "Start here" language on primary CTA
- Clear separation: try-before-commit vs. deep commitment

---

### **STEP 2: PRODUCT EXPLORATION & FIRST PURCHASE**
**Location:** Product Pages → Collections  
**Primary Goal:** Drive single purchase while hinting at collectibility

**Flow (Single Product Page):**
```
Product Page (e.g., Washington Blend)
  ↓
Product Details + Reviews + Brew Guide
  ↓
Add to Cart / Subscribe Toggle
  ↓
NextStepBridge Component ← NEW
  ("Loved Washington? Never miss another blend")
  ↓
Link to Torch Box Page
  ↓
Founder Story + Collection Progress
  ↓
Reviews + Testimonials
  ↓
Final CTA: "Collect All Founders"
```

**Components Involved:**
- FounderProductPage: Full product experience
- AddToCartButton: Single purchase
- SubscribeToggle: Early subscription option
- **NextStepBridge:** Step 2 → Step 3 transition
- FoundersCollectionProgress: Collectible system visualization
- ReviewsSection: Social proof

**Reinforcement:**
- "Build Your Set" messaging on collection pages
- "Collect All Six" emphasis
- Product pages show that each blend "retires forever"
- Reviews emphasize quality and legacy

---

### **STEP 3: COLLECTION & MEMBERSHIP**
**Location:** Torch Box Landing Page  
**Primary Goal:** Convert single-purchase customers to subscribers

**Flow:**
```
Torch Box Landing Page
  ↓
FomoBanner: "Limited monthly spots available"
  ↓
Hero: "Become a Torchbearer" + Membership Benefits
  ↓
Exclusive Blends Messaging: "Not available in store"
  ↓
"More Than Coffee. A System" → Collector system explained
  ↓
Value Comparison Table: Savings vs. retail
  ↓
Tier Selection (Ember / Beacon / Blaze)
  ↓
Torchbearer Card: Membership value
  ↓
Social Proof: 200+ subscribers, 4.8 rating
  ↓
Final CTA: "Claim Your Box Today"
  ↓
Navigation Links Back to Collection / Home
```

**Components Involved:**
- TorchBoxSection (homepage): Step 2 → Step 3 bridge
- FirstOrderBonus: Exclusive first-box incentive
- TheTorchBox landing: Full subscription experience
- TorchbearerCard: Membership tangibility
- ValueComparisonTable: Clear ROI
- FoundersCollectionProgress: Collection tracking

**Reinforcement:**
- "Step 3: Complete Your Membership" messaging
- "Ready to Collect All Six Founders?"
- First-box exclusive bonus (perceived value)
- Limited availability (scarcity)
- Member-only blends (exclusivity)

## Key Journey Features

### **1. Clear Step Labeling**
- **Step 1:** Featured Product (Homepage)
- **Step 2:** Collection Exploration + Product Purchase (Product/Collection Pages)
- **Step 3:** Membership (Torch Box Landing)

Each step reinforces where the customer is in their journey.

### **2. NextStepBridge Component** ⭐ NEW
**Purpose:** Connect product pages directly to Torch Box
- Appears after product details on every product page
- Addresses: "You loved [product]? Here's how to collect them all"
- CTAs users from single purchases → membership
- Reinforces scarcity: "Each blend retires forever"

### **3. Collectible System Reinforcement**
- Progress visualization (6-item series)
- "Collect All X" messaging on every page
- "Retires forever" scarcity language
- Limited monthly availability urgency

### **4. Multiple Re-entry Points**
- Homepage → Single product → Torch Box (traditional path)
- Homepage → Torch Box directly (power users)
- Collections → Product → Torch Box (explorers)
- Product page → Back to collections (browsers)

Each page links back to maintain navigation flow.

### **5. Consistent Brand Language**
- "Built for Those Who Carry the Flame"
- "More Than Coffee. A System"
- "Never miss a blend"
- "Collect the Founders"
- Confident, intentional tone throughout

## No Dead Ends

Every page has a clear next action:
- ✅ Home → Product or Torch Box
- ✅ Product → Cart, Subscribe, or Torch Box
- ✅ Collection → Product or Torch Box
- ✅ Torch Box → Purchase or Claim

No isolated pages or confusion about what to do next.

## Summary

**The journey transforms visitors into:**
1. **Curious browsers** → Homepage discovery
2. **Single buyers** → First product purchase
3. **Collectors** → Torch Box subscription

With consistent messaging, clear step progression, and natural conversion points at each stage. The collectible system is reinforced throughout, making each stage feel intentional and part of a larger, more rewarding system.
